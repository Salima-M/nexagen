About NexaGen

We are a collaborative space for people who want to understand life through data. Students and researchers work side by side, share methods, and turn questions in biology into results they can test.

  • Who we areA community of students and researchers.
  • What drives usData-driven discovery in bioinformatics.
  • How we workOpenly, together, and with room to learn.

What we do

Three ways to take part in NexaGen.

Learn

Workshops and study groups on sequence analysis, genomics tools, and programming for biology.

Collaborate

Joint research projects where members share data, code, and ideas across disciplines.

Innovate

Build tools and pipelines that make biological data easier to analyze and understand.
